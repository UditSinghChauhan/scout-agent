# Zoho Corporation: Sales Prospect Brief for Campus Hiring-Challenge Platform

## Snapshot

- Zoho Corporation is a global software company headquartered in Chennai, India, with over 18,000 employees as of 2025. [1]
- The company operates offices in over 80 countries, including the US (Del Valle, Texas), China, Singapore, and Japan, with bulk operations centered in Chennai. [1]

## Buying signals

- Active job postings increased to 162 in 2026, up from 119 in 2025, indicating sustained hiring activity. [2]
- The company is undergoing significant workforce expansion, including new rural campuses and operations in Kerala, which likely drives increased demand for graduate talent. [3][4]

## Pain points

- Zoho relies on its own internal assessment platform for coding and logical reasoning tests, which may lack the specialized gamification or challenge-based engagement features of a dedicated campus hiring platform. [5]
- Managing recruitment across multiple geographies (Chennai, Salem, Coimbatore, Tirunelveli, Madurai, and new rural sites) likely creates logistical complexity in coordinating campus and off-campus drives. [3][6]

## Who to approach (roles only)

- Talent Acquisition Manager is a confirmed role within the HR function. [7]
- Senior decision-makers for campus recruitment or engineering hiring (e.g., Head of Campus Recruitment, Director of Talent Acquisition) are not explicitly named in public sources, but a dedicated HR/campus-recruitment function exists. [7][8]

## Pitch angle

- Position the platform as a scalable solution to handle the volume of fresher hiring across Zoho's expanding rural and urban campuses, reducing the load on their internal assessment tools. [3][5][6]
- Highlight the ability to standardize technical assessments (coding/logic) across multiple locations like Chennai, Salem, and Coimbatore to ensure consistent quality in a decentralized hiring model. [5][6]

## Risks

- Zoho is a product company that builds its own HR tools (Zoho Recruit) and internal assessment platforms, making them a 'build vs. buy' risk. [5][8]
- Lack of public data on specific university partnerships or the exact scale of annual campus programs makes it difficult to quantify the total addressable market for campus-specific challenges. [9]
