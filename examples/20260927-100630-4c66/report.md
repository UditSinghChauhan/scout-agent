## Company in 60 seconds

- Zoho operates primarily on a SaaS (Software as a Service) cloud business model, providing a wide suite of business software and office productivity tools. [1]
- The company maintains a core philosophy of sustainable growth without VC funding, focusing on fair pricing for products rather than chasing investor returns. [2]
- Zoho's US headquarters was moved from Pleasanton, California to Del Valle, Texas in 2019. [3]

## Tech stack

- Zoho relies heavily on Java for its enterprise-grade functionality and backend architecture. [1]
- The company utilizes C and C++ for performance-critical modules like indexing engines and high-throughput components. [1]

## News worth mentioning

- Zoho is recognized as an Overall Leader in both the Customer Experience and Vendor Credibility models in Dresner's 2026 Wisdom of Crowds BI Market Study. [4]
- In 2026, Zoho Notebook celebrates its 10th anniversary, marking a decade of building user workflows and productivity tools. [5]
