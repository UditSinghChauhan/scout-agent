# Zoho SDE Intern Interview Prep

_Purpose: Interview prep · Run `20260927-175700-2f13`_

**Key takeaways**

- Zoho’s interview process is highly technical, requiring mastery of data structures, algorithms, and system design, with a strong emphasis on coding problem solving. [1][2][3]
- Zoho’s tech stack is diverse, centered on Java, Python, Node.js, and React, and it runs its own data centers powered by solar farms. [4][5][6]
- Recent AI initiatives—agentic AI upgrades and new ML tools—highlight Zoho’s focus on in‑house AI capabilities, which may surface in interview questions or project discussions. [7][8][9]

## Company in 60 seconds

- Zoho is a global SaaS company that builds its entire product suite in-house, hosting all services on its own data centers powered by solar farms. [6]

## Tech stack

- Backend development uses Java (Spring Boot, Spring MVC, Struts, Vaadin) and Python (Django, Flask, etc.) with Node.js frameworks (Express, Koa, Fastify). [4][5]
- Frontend is primarily React, with mobile apps built in Flutter. [5]
- Zoho owns and operates four global data centers, including new locations in the Netherlands and Ireland. [4]

## News worth mentioning

- Zoho launched free agentic AI upgrades across its core business applications, extending AI capabilities (Zia) to multiple products at no additional cost. [7]
- Zoho Creator introduced Zoho DataPrep, an AI-powered self-service data preparation service integrated into BI & Analytics for ETL workflows, plus augmented analytics with AI/ML features. [9]

## Likely topics

- Coding rounds will test proficiency in data structures and algorithms; expect five problems per round, with only those solving all five advancing. [1][2]
- System‑design questions may involve building an application using Zoho’s Catalyst platform or designing a scalable service for its SaaS products. [2][3]
- Interviewers may probe knowledge of Zoho’s in‑house AI tools (QuickML, ConvoKraft) and its proprietary data‑center architecture. [4]

## Smart questions to ask

- What opportunities exist for interns to contribute to Zoho’s recent AI initiatives, such as agentic AI or QuickML? [7][9]
- Can you describe the typical workflow for a new feature from ideation to deployment within Zoho’s proprietary development ecosystem? [10]
