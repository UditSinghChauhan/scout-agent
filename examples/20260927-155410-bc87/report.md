**Key takeaways**

- Their existing recruitment stack (Zoho Recruit) integrates with HackerEarth assessments, showing openness to third‑party testing tools and a need for scalable campus‑hiring solutions. [1][2]
- Key risk is that Zoho already has in‑house assessment features and no publicly identified senior owners of campus hiring, which may slow adoption and decision‑making. [3][4]

## Snapshot

- The company operates in more than 80 countries with offices and data centers across Asia, Europe, and the United States. [5]
- Zoho runs its own applicant tracking system, Zoho Recruit, which supports candidate sourcing, workflow automation, and pre‑screening assessments. [1][3]
- Zoho explicitly defines and engages in campus recruitment as a hiring channel. [6]

## Buying signals

- Zoho Recruit integrates with HackerEarth assessments, indicating willingness to adopt external technical testing platforms. [2]
- Pre‑screening assessments can be embedded directly in Zoho Recruit application forms, showing a need for scalable assessment workflows. [3]
- The company’s recruitment process involves up to five interview rounds, suggesting a high volume of candidates that could benefit from automated challenge‑based screening. [7]

## Pain points

- Lack of publicly identified senior owners for campus hiring may lead to fragmented decision‑making and slower rollout of new tools. [4]

## Pitch angle

- Position the platform as a seamless extension to Zoho Recruit, offering customizable, brandable coding challenges that integrate via Zoho’s existing API, reducing manual grading and accelerating campus pipelines. [1][2][3]
- Zoho’s analytics and benchmarking features deliver data‑driven insights across its footprint of offices in over 80 countries, supporting its “We don’t just hire talent. We invest in people” hiring philosophy. [5]

## Risks

- Integration effort with Zoho Recruit’s API may require custom development, raising implementation cost concerns. [1]
